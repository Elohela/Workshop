THE BIRTH OF AN ICON

April 17, 1964

On a spring day that would change automotive history forever, Ford introduced the Mustang at the New York World's Fair. The vision of Lee Iacocca and his team, the Mustang was designed to be affordable, stylish, and fun to drive.

The Pony Car Revolution

The Mustang created an entirely new segment of automobiles known as "pony cars" - affordable, compact, highly styled cars with a sporty or performance-oriented image. It was an instant success, selling over 22,000 units on its first day and over one million in its first 18 months.

Cultural Impact

More than just a car, the Mustang became a symbol of American freedom, youth, and independence. It appeared in countless movies, became a favorite of celebrities, and captured the imagination of generations of drivers.

50 YEARS OF EVOLUTION

1964-1966

First Generation

The original Mustang debuts with a long hood and short deck design. Available as a coupe or convertible, it featured the iconic running horse emblem and became an instant classic.

1967-1973

Growing Muscle

The Mustang grows larger and more powerful. The legendary Shelby GT500 and Boss 429 models are born, becoming some of the most sought-after muscle cars in history.

1974-1978

Mustang II

Responding to the oil crisis, Ford introduces a smaller, more fuel-efficient Mustang. Despite criticism, it kept the nameplate alive during challenging times.

1979-1993

Fox Body Era

The Fox platform brings back performance with the 5.0 V8. The Mustang GT becomes a performance bargain and dominates street racing culture.

1994-2004

The SN95/New Edge

A modern redesign brings flowing curves and the return of the legendary Cobra nameplate. The Mustang maintains its position as America's favorite sports car.

2005-2014

Retro Revolution

Ford looks to the past for inspiration, creating a modern Mustang with classic styling cues. The supercharged GT500 produces over 662 horsepower.

2014

50th Anniversary

Ford celebrates 50 years of the Mustang with special edition models and unveils the all-new sixth generation for 2015, featuring independent rear suspension and global availability.
